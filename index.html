    How to play Kadi

    On your turn play a card that matches the top card by suit or rank, or draw one — drawing ends your turn, even if the card you draw is playable. Same-rank cards can go down together, in any order, as long as one of them matches.

    2 / 3Next player draws 2 / 3 — unless they block with a matching 2 or 3, a Joker or an Ace. Penalties stack.
    JokerDraw 5. Plays on anything.
    APlays on anything and lets you request a suit. Also cancels a penalty.
    JJump — skips the next player (with two players you play again). Each Jump in a set skips one more player, but never you.
    KKickback — reverses direction. Each K reverses once, so two in one play cancel out. With two players, a K sends play straight back to you.
    Q / 8Question — answer it right away with a 4–10 of the same suit, or draw 1.

    Niko Kadi: with 2 cards or fewer, tap 📢 Niko Kadi before you play your last card.

    To win, go out on a 4–10, or a Q/8 you answered. You can't finish on A, 2, 3, J, K or a Joker.
